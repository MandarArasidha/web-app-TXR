Intent

How Lenscase works

  • Open with one hand

    Hold it in one hand and press gently.

  • Place your eyewear upside down

    Lay your glasses in, upside down, so the frame rests inside.

  • Close and carry

    Close it with a magnetic click and carry as you like.
